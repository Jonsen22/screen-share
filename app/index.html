Welcome to screen-share

Lightweight, Discord-style screen sharing for Windows — nothing else.

v0.1.0
  • Direct P2P & Encrypted

    Video and audio stream directly between peers using DTLS-SRTP encryption. The signaling server only handles room codes and WebRTC handshakes.

  • Isolated Per-App Audio (WASAPI)

    Only the Windows application you pick is captured — never your system notifications or Discord voice chat.

  • Quick Tips & Shortcuts

    Hosts can switch screens, quality, or audio source live without closing the room. While watching, press I for the telemetry HUD and F (or double-click) for fullscreen.

Choose a Nickname

Your nickname is saved locally and shown to others in the room. You can change it anytime from the top bar when not in a room.

Discord Share Webhook

Paste a Discord channel webhook URL to post your live room link in 1 click. It is stored locally on your PC and never sent to any third-party server.

In Discord: Channel Settings → Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook → Copy Webhook URL.

Ban Viewer?

Are you sure you want to ban this viewer? They will be removed immediately and their IP address will be blocked from rejoining for the rest of this room session.

Share Your Screen in This Room

Start sharing your screen alongside the room host. Others in the room can click your stream pill to watch you live.

Pick a single app or "All PC Audio (Exclude Discord)". Tip: mute the watched stream when capturing all PC audio to avoid echo.
